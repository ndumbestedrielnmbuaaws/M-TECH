Mobile app · M-TECH STUDIO

M TECH Remote

Control M-TECH STUDIO from your phone, from anywhere in the world. See the Program screen live and run your show without touching the computer.

  • All studio controls on your phone: inputs, cut and transitions, audio and recording.
  • Live Program screen, so you always see what the audience sees.
  • Connect from anywhere, even when the phone and the studio are in different countries.
  • Secure pairing with an ID and password, using Phone Connect in the studio's Settings.
Download forAndroid Open in SafariiPhone

Free download. Works with M-TECH STUDIO on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file M-TECH-Remote.apk.
  3. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  4. Open M TECH Remote, then in M-TECH STUDIO go to Settings → Phone Connect and enter the ID and password shown on your phone.

Install on iPhone

  1. On your iPhone open Safari and tap Open in Safari above.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open M TECH Remote from your home screen, type your ID number and password, and tap Phone Connect.
  4. In M-TECH STUDIO go to Settings → Phone Connect, type the same ID and password and press Connect.

It works like the Android app and needs no App Store. A store version is planned for later.
